Our Story

Republic Road Schools has been a cornerstone of quality education in Community 4, Tema for over 15 years. Established with a vision to provide accessible, high-quality junior high school education, our institution has grown into one of the most respected educational centers in the Greater Accra Region. We pride ourselves on our commitment to academic excellence, character development, and the holistic growth of our students.

Our Mission, Vision & Motto

Motto: AIM HIGH

Mission: To provide accessible, high-quality Junior High School education through disciplined instruction, modern pedagogy, and holistic training that prepares students for success in their academic and personal endeavors.

Vision: To be the leading junior high school in the Tema metropolis, raising knowledgeable, innovative, ethical, and upright leaders for Ghana and the global community.

Core Values & Philosophy

Our Core Values:

  • Hard Work: Dedication and perseverance in all endeavors
  • Integrity: Honesty and ethical conduct in all dealings
  • Perseverance: Never giving up despite challenges
  • Respect: Valuing diversity and treating all with dignity

Our Philosophy: Guided by our crest's flaming torch symbolizing enlightenment, we believe in illuminating young minds through rigorous academics, technological innovation, character development, and preparation for responsible citizenship in a rapidly changing world.

Academic Overview

Our Junior High School curriculum strictly conforms to official Ghana Education Service (GES) standards, ensuring students receive standardized quality education. Our comprehensive program focuses on rigorous preparation for the BECE (Basic Education Certificate Examination) while nurturing critical thinking, creativity, and practical skills through our balanced approach to academics and character education.

Admission Requirements

Required Documents:

  1. Entrance Examination: Readiness assessment in Core Mathematics and English (or equivalent transcript)
  2. Primary School Records: Transcript/cumulative record from Basic 6 (or final primary leaving school report)
  3. Medical Report: Health clearance from a certified healthcare facility (vaccination record)
  4. Birth Certificate: Copy of authentic birth certificate or national ID

Admission Guidelines

  • Entry Level: Basic 7 (JHS 1), Basic 8 (JHS 2), Basic 9 (JHS 3)
  • Age Requirement: Minimum 12 years old for JHS 1
  • Capacity: Limited spaces - early application recommended
  • Selection: Merit-based on entrance exam & records

📌 Important: Application deadlines are typically in July/August for September intake. We recommend contacting our admission office for current deadlines.
